### Post 1 — X thread

**Source:** 3 AM waking / cortisol-blood-sugar article  
**CTA:** Quiz

1/ You wake up at 3 AM and your brain is suddenly wide awake.

That does not always mean “you’re stressed.”

Sometimes it means your body just fired the wrong signal at the wrong time.

2/ Two common triggers:

- Cortisol spikes too early
- Blood sugar dips and your body compensates

Either one can pull you out of sleep and make your brain feel annoyingly alert.

3/ The mistake is treating every 3 AM wake-up the same way.

More melatonin is not always the move.

A better question: what pattern are you in?

4/ If you fall asleep fine but wake up at 2-4 AM, your problem may not be sleep onset. It may be sleep maintenance.

Different problem. Different fix.
