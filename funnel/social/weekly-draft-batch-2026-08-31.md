## Draft 3 — Standalone post

**Format:** Standalone X/LinkedIn-style post  
**CTA:** Supporting article  
**Destination:** `https://sleepsmarter.io/blog/sleep-for-athletes`

Sleep is not the opposite of training.

It is part of training.

If you train hard and sleep six hours, do not be shocked when performance stalls, soreness lingers, and every workout feels heavier than it should.

Before you chase a new supplement stack, check the boring stuff:

- Consistent wake time
- Enough sleep opportunity
- Caffeine cut off early enough
- Cool, dark room
- Real wind-down after late training
- Naps that do not wreck bedtime

Recovery does not need to look impressive.

It needs to work repeatedly.

Full guide: https://sleepsmarter.io/blog/sleep-for-athletes
